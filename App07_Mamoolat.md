## 1. Overview

Mamoolat ("we," "our," or "the App") helps a seeker (*salik*) keep a daily record of their prescribed devotions (*mamoolat*: wird, tasbih, prayers, Quran tilawat, muraqabah), review the month (*muhasabah*) and send a monthly report (*karguzari*) to their Shaykh or Khanqah. We respect your privacy and collect only what is needed for these features.

> We do **not** sell personal data, display third-party advertisements, or use behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Usage Without an Account (Guest Access)
You can use every daily-tracking feature without signing in. Your records are then stored **only on your device** and nothing is sent to us.

### B. Account Information (Optional Google Sign-In)
If you choose to sign in with Google (to back up your records, use them on another device, or let your Khanqah see your progress), Firebase Authentication processes:
* Email address
* Display name and profile photo (from your Google account)
* Authentication User ID (UID) and sign-in provider metadata

### C. Profile Details You Choose to Enter
All optional, and synced only when you are signed in:
* Your name and city / place
* Your phone / WhatsApp number and country
* Your silsila (tariqah), Shaykh, Khanqah, and the Khanqah's WhatsApp number or email

### D. Your Daily Records and Monthly Notes
When signed in, the following are stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they sync across your devices and can be reviewed by your Khanqah:
* Daily records of your mamoolat (prayers, counts, timings, muraqabah minutes, Quran progress)
* Your mamoolat list and Quran khatm position
* Monthly reflections, questions for your Shaykh, personal-meeting requests, and the Khanqah's replies
* The date and time you last opened the App

### E. Locally Stored Data (On-Device)
The App stores your records, settings (language, Hijri date adjustment, reminders) and an offline copy of the App in your device's local storage so it works without an internet connection. You can clear it at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.

---

## 3. Who Can See Your Data

Synced data is visible only to **you** and to the **administrators of your Khanqah's Mamoolat dashboard** (your Shaykh and the people he appoints), so they can guide you and reply to your questions. It is not shown to other users and is not shared with anyone else.

---

## 4. Reports, Backups & Sharing

* **Karguzari PDF, JSON backup and CSV export** are generated locally on your device.
* When you send a report (for example via WhatsApp or email) the App only opens your device's share sheet; the app you choose handles the message under its own privacy policy.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

* **Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):** optional sign-in and cloud storage / sync. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy & Security documentation.
* **Google Sign-In / Android Credential Manager:** shows your Google accounts on the device so you can sign in.
* **Google Play In-App Updates:** checks Google Play for a newer version of the App.
* **Google Fonts:** loads the App's typefaces (*Amiri*, *Scheherazade New*, *EB Garamond*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*). Font requests may include standard request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent).

---

## 6. Device Permissions

* **Internet:** to sync your records and sign in.
* **Credentials / biometric (`USE_CREDENTIALS`, `USE_BIOMETRIC`):** used only by Google's account picker when you sign in (for example to confirm with a fingerprint or passkey). The App itself never reads your fingerprint.

The App does **not** access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or call logs.

---

## 7. Data Security & Retention

* All communication between the App and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
* Synced data is kept while your account is active and deleted on request (see below).

---

## 8. Your Rights & Account / Data Deletion

You can:
* Use the App without an account at any time.
* Edit or remove your profile details on the Nisbat page.
* Sign out at any time from Settings.
* Delete records on your device (Settings → Reset all daily logs) or uninstall the App.
* **Delete your account inside the App:** Settings → Account → **Delete my account**. After you confirm with Google, your sign-in account and all synced data (profile, mamoolat list, daily records, monthly notes and questions) are deleted immediately from the cloud and from your device.
* Or request deletion by emailing [azim.zaytunweb@gmail.com](mailto:azim.zaytunweb@gmail.com) from the Google account you signed in with. Email requests are completed within **30 days**.
* Nothing is kept after deletion, except that a report you already sent through WhatsApp or email stays with its recipient.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Mamoolat is a general-audience religious application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.
