# Privacy Policy for 40 Durud o Salam (Chahal Durood-Salaam)

**Last updated:** August 02, 2026

Thank you for using **40 Durud o Salam** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Usage

**40 Durud o Salam** is designed as a privacy-focused, offline-first Islamic reading application.

* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifier).
* **No Account Registration:** You do not need to create an account or log in to use the app.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** The app does not utilize any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools to track your behavior.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All preferences and reading progress are stored strictly **locally on your device** using your device's local storage capabilities:

* **Last Read Position & Bookmarks:** Saved on your device so you can resume reading where you left off.
* **App Preferences:** Language selection, dark/light theme preferences, and page display modes.

This data never leaves your device and is erased if you clear the application data or uninstall the application.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The app requests only minimal, non-sensitive permissions required for core functionality:

* **Keep Awake (`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`):** Prevents your screen from turning off or locking while you are reading or reciting Durood Shareef.
* **Storage Access:** Used solely to access bundled PDF content internally within the application sandbox.

No permissions for Camera, Microphone, Precise Location, Contacts, or Device Storage files are requested or required.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services and External Links

The application operates completely offline and does not contain advertisements or integrations with third-party tracking services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we comply fully with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA and GDPR).

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
