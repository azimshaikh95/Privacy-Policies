# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-28

### Introduction
Thank you for using Sunan wa Aadab, a reader for the book *Sunan wa ʾĀdāb* by Muftī Abū Bakr ibn Muṣṭafā Patnī in English, Gujarati and Urdu. We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

### Information Collection and Use
Sunan wa Aadab does not collect, store, transmit or share any personally identifiable information (PII). There are no user accounts and no sign-in. The whole book is included in the app, and the app works fully offline.

### Data Stored on Your Device
The following is stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage) and never leaves it on its own:
- your bookmarks (points and chapters);
- text errors you report from inside the app, and their notes;
- your last reading position;
- your settings (language, text size, theme, colour, PDF options).

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

### Sharing and Feedback
Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it yourself – for example when you copy or share a point or chapter, save or share a chapter as a PDF, or send feedback (which may include the text errors you reported). These are sent through the app you choose, such as WhatsApp, email or your phone's share sheet, and are then handled under that app's own privacy policy. Feedback you send to us is used only to reply to you and to correct the text of the book.

### Third-Party Services, Analytics and Advertising
The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect usage data.

### Permissions
The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or other personal data. Saving a PDF writes the file to your device's Documents folder when you ask it to.

### Children's Privacy
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
