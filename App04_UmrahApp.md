# Privacy Policy for Umrah Companion

**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026

**Effective Date:** October 10, 2026

Welcome to **Umrah Companion** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information displayed, accessed, and processed through our mobile application (**Umrah Companion**, the "App").

The App serves as a digital companion and viewer for pilgrims, designed to make logistical details, flight tickets, hotel reservations, and travel documents easily accessible during their Umrah journey.

---

## 1. Our Role and Nature of the App

* **No Direct Account Creation:** We do not collect personal registration details—such as your name, personal email address, phone number, profile pictures, or passwords—directly through the App.
* **Tour Operator Managed Data:** All pilgrim profiles, logistical details, flight/travel itineraries, document links, and hotel arrangements are pre-provisioned and managed directly by your **registered Umrah tour operator**.
* **View-Only Logistics Utility:** The App functions primarily as an informational viewer so that pilgrims can reference their itinerary, hotel vouchers, transport schedules, and related travel links on the go.

---

## 2. Information Displayed and Handled in the App

### A. Pre-Provisioned Pilgrimage Information
Your registered tour operator inputs and manages the necessary travel data into the tour management system, which may be displayed to you within the App:
* **Travel & Ticketing Information:** Flight details, departure/arrival schedules, bus/transfer logistics, and seat assignments.
* **Accommodation Details:** Hotel names, addresses, room bookings, and check-in/check-out dates in Makkah and Madinah.
* **Document Links & Itineraries:** Secure links or references to travel permits, visa copies, insurance information, and daily itinerary schedules.
* **Emergency & Group Contacts:** Contact details for your group leader, guide, or tour operator representatives.

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data
To ensure stability and display relevant local utilities, the App may automatically collect limited technical information:
* **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Device model, operating system version, app crash reports, and system performance metrics to diagnose technical bugs.
* **Approximate Location (Optional):** If enabled, approximate device location may be used on-device solely to help you navigate to your assigned hotel or locate group meeting points. We do not track or store your persistent location history.

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
* We do **not** prompt you to create an independent public profile.
* We do **not** collect credit card numbers, payment information, or financial details.
* We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal or travel data to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

---

## 3. How We Use the Information

The information made available via the App is strictly used to:
1. Display your verified travel itinerary, hotel accommodations, and transport schedules.
2. Enable easy one-tap access to your essential travel documents and vouchers.
3. Assist you in finding your designated hotels, transport pickup points, and tour operator contact points during your pilgrimage.
4. Diagnose technical glitches and optimize app performance.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

Depending on your operating system and optional features used, the App may request the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Optional / Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Storage / Files (Read Only)** | To download or open your itinerary, visa documents, or hotel vouchers locally on your device. | Optional |
| **Location (While in Use)** | To assist you in displaying local directions to your designated hotel or meeting point. | Optional |
| **Notifications** | To receive important operational alerts from your tour operator (e.g., bus departure timing, gate changes). | Optional |

*You can enable or disable these permissions at any time via your device settings.*

---

## 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not disclose your information to third parties, except in the following limited situations:
* **Your Registered Tour Operator:** Your tour operator manages your tour data and uses our platform to distribute logistics to your device.
* **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** Highly secure cloud hosting services (such as AWS or Google Cloud) used solely to store and deliver the encrypted itinerary data.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by mandatory legal processes, national security regulations, or law enforcement requests within your jurisdiction or the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

---

## 6. Data Security and Access Control

* **Access Credentials:** Access to your group or individual itinerary is typically provided via a secure booking reference, voucher code, or access credentials issued directly to you by your tour operator.
* **Encryption:** Data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption.
* **Retention:** Travel records and logistics displayed in the App are maintained according to the active duration of your Umrah package and the data retention policies agreed upon with your registered tour operator.

---

## 7. Data Rights and Inquiries

Because your booking information, ticket details, and hotel arrangements are provided and controlled by your **registered tour operator**, any requests to modify, update, or delete your travel information should be directed to your tour operator.

If you have technical questions regarding the App itself or wish to request the removal of cached app data from your device, you may contact us directly.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App provides travel and logistics information for travelers of all ages under the supervision and booking arrangement of an adult or registered tour operator. We do not independently collect personal information directly from minors.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our logistical viewer or legal requirements. Any modifications will be indicated with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
